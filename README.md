# 🌱 Eco-Race

Eco-Race is a mobile application designed to transform environmental awareness into real action through gamification, rewards, and competition.

---

## 📌 Overview

Environmental awareness alone is not enough. Many people understand environmental issues but do not take consistent action.

Eco-Race addresses this problem by introducing a system where users complete eco-friendly tasks, earn points, and compete through leaderboards at both individual and institutional levels.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

* Users register and access the dashboard
* Select eco-friendly tasks
* Complete activities and upload proof
* The system verifies submissions
* Points are awarded
* Leaderboards are updated (users & institutions)

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* Gamification (points, rewards, and leaderboards)
* Competition between schools and universities
* Verification system (proof-based validation)
* AI-based task suggestions and environmental news
* Progress tracking and rewards system

---

## 📊 Expected Impact

* Increases participation in eco-friendly activities
* Encourages long-term environmental behavior
* Creates measurable environmental impact
* Promotes teamwork and competition

---

## 🧠 Innovation

Eco-Race combines gamification, verification, and institutional competition into a single system, making environmental action engaging, trackable, and credible.
